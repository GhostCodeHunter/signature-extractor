What photo restore does, in order

Each step is plain image processing. Here is a faded, scratched sample after every stage.

  1. Low contrast, a colour cast, dust and scratches.

  2. Each colour channel is stretched to its own range.

  3. Specks and thin scratches are filled from the pixels around them.

  4. Grain is averaged away, guided by the edges so they stay put.

  5. Edges get a final lift, and colour a gentle boost.

Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Both tools run in your browser on your device. The photo is never sent to a server, and there is no account.

How does it handle a watermark on the photo?

With watermark removal on Auto, marks that are fainter than the pen or a different colour from it are left out, and the notice under the controls says how many were found. Pen strokes away from a mark are not changed, but a mark that crosses the pen can leave a small notch. A watermark in the same colour and darkness as the pen cannot be told apart, so use Off, or click the leftovers to erase them.

Does photo restore use AI?

Partly. The base is classic image processing: tone correction, speck repair, noise reduction, sharpening and an optional flat background colour, which cleans what is in the photo without inventing anything. On top of that there is an optional AI face detail step. It uses a face-restoration network that runs in your browser to redraw the eyes, brows, lips and skin of a small or blurry face. Your photo is not uploaded, but the first time you switch it on the page downloads a model of about 76 MB and keeps it. It takes no text prompt. Because the face is redrawn, small features can change, so compare the result with your original, and do not use it for documents that must show an unaltered photo.

Will it change the faces in my photo?

Only if you turn on AI face detail, which redraws the face from a learned model of faces and can alter small features. With it off, nothing is redrawn: face protection gives detected faces lighter noise reduction and sharpening and no colour boost, and Strict mode only corrects tone and resizes. The finder works best on faces that look at the camera, so you can mark any it misses.

Why does 8× not look like a sharper original?

Enlarging makes the pixel grid bigger and the edges cleaner. It cannot recover fine texture the camera never recorded, so very large sizes look smoother, not more detailed.

What size of photo can I use?

Photos up to 24 megapixels open, and the saved file can be up to 36 megapixels. Larger enlargements are switched off for big photos.
